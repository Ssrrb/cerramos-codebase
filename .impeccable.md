## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are business owners and entrepreneurs who already sell through WhatsApp and Instagram and need a clearer, lower-friction way to turn conversations into structured orders. In auth and onboarding moments, they need to understand the next step quickly, trust the product immediately, and avoid any feeling that setup will be complicated.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel easy to follow, modern, and trust-inspiring. Cerramos should come across as operationally sharp, calm under pressure, and practical for real businesses rather than generic SaaS theater.

### Aesthetic Direction
Favor clean, guided layouts with strong hierarchy, restrained motion, and a professional product feel. Keep the UI current and polished, but do not overdesign MVP surfaces. Use visual cues that reinforce clarity, progress, and confidence for commerce workflows.

### Design Principles
1. Make the next action obvious immediately.
2. Earn trust through clarity, consistency, and calm visual structure.
3. Prefer practical polish over decorative complexity.
4. Keep auth and onboarding flows lightweight for busy operators on desktop or mobile.
5. Use modern components and states that feel responsive without drawing attention to themselves.
6. Use styles.css for typography, color, and layout(Do not use tailwind directly).

### Quality Bar
Target MVP polish: clean, credible, and cohesive, with attention to spacing, states, and copy, but without flagship-level visual experimentation.
