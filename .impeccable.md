## Design Context

### Users
Cerramos is built for small and medium merchants who already sell through WhatsApp and Instagram but still close orders manually. Their operational context is fast, mobile-heavy, and interruption-prone: they need structured orders, clearer payment state, and an internal panel that reduces back-and-forth rather than adding process.

The buyer-facing experience should help shoppers complete a purchase with minimal friction from a social link. The merchant-facing experience should help operators review, confirm, and manage orders with speed and confidence.

### Brand Personality
Cerramos should feel geometrical, modern, and minimalistic. The emotional target is confidence first, with a secondary layer of friendliness, safety, and kindness. Interfaces should feel calm and competent rather than flashy or salesy.

### Aesthetic Direction
The desired visual direction combines:

- admin/dashboard clarity and fit from Birdseye, where dense information still feels ordered and approachable
- the modern, safe, kind tone of Claude
- the calm, high-trust checkout feel of Stripe Checkout for buyer flows

The product should support both light and dark themes. Avoid neon colors and any palette choices that create distrust. Avoid interfaces that feel overwhelming, complicated, or weird.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize trust over novelty. Every screen should feel dependable, legible, and operationally clear before it feels expressive.
- Keep geometry disciplined. Use structured spacing, clean alignment, and restrained shapes so dense flows still feel calm.
- Make complex operations feel simple. Reduce visible friction, reveal secondary detail progressively, and avoid cluttered decision points.
- Design for friendly efficiency. Merchant tools should feel fast and capable; buyer flows should feel safe, direct, and reassuring.
- Use color conservatively. Support light and dark mode, but avoid neon or attention-seeking accents that weaken product trust.
